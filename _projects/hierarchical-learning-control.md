This research develops multi-level and multi-objective decision architectures for systems whose local controllers must act quickly while a higher-level coordinator protects shared constraints and system-wide objectives.

Applications include:

- Coordinated EV fast charging under distribution-grid constraints
- Multi-domain smart-city management across buildings, mobility, and healthcare
- Safe reinforcement learning with feasibility projection and action shielding
- Sensitivity analysis for communication-efficient coordination
- Adaptive resource allocation across coupled learning agents

The objective is to combine the adaptability of reinforcement learning with the structure, feasibility, and interpretability expected from control and optimization.
