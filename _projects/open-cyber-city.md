OpenCyberCity is a 1:12-scale modular smart-city testbed developed at Virginia Commonwealth University. It supports realistic experiments across buildings, transportation, energy, manufacturing, healthcare, robotics, sensing, networking, and edge intelligence.

My contributions connect the full research pipeline:

- Sensing and embedded-system integration
- Middleware, data pipelines, dashboards, and monitoring
- Forecasting, anomaly detection, optimization, and control
- Closed-loop experimental validation
- Student mentorship, publications, posters, and proposal development

The platform creates a bridge between mathematical and learning-based methods and the realities of networked physical infrastructure. Learn more at [occtestbed.com](https://occtestbed.com/).
