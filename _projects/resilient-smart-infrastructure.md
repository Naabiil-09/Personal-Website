Reliable cyber-physical systems need more than an accurate prediction model. They require trustworthy sensor streams, calibrated uncertainty, operational constraints, interpretable decisions, and robust fallback behavior.

This program integrates:

- Bayesian and deep-learning forecasts for energy and demand response
- Data-quality monitoring and anomaly detection
- Risk-aware model predictive control and safe reinforcement learning
- Smart-building temperature, security, and energy management
- Microgrid and distributed-energy optimization under uncertainty
- Adaptive control for smart water-distribution systems

Performance is evaluated through system-level outcomes—including safety, efficiency, resilience, and constraint satisfaction—not prediction accuracy alone.
